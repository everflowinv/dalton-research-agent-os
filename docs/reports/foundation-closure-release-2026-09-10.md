## Closure scope

This release keeps model cost accounting conservative, including unknown paid
outcomes and historical corrections without free headroom. It restores
cross-scope credential bindings, preserves transcript evidence semantics, and
keeps issuer extraction and statement governance fail closed. Dossier drafting
excludes retired Claims before its prompt bound while leaving historical reads
unchanged. Forecast recovery separates the filed revenue anchor from economic
drivers and appends one specification-bound version in the existing company
model chain when the authorized specification contract changes. Old model and
forecast records remain immutable, ordinary same-spec actualisation is
unchanged, and repeated current identities remain idempotent.
