# Deep Insight company fairness

Deep Insight scheduling now considers screened companies in mission priority order and gives each company its own input and permission identity. A content refusal for one company therefore remains terminal for that exact input while the next eligible company can run.

The source fingerprint binds the current dossier, debate map, prior gate and human decision, plus the same bounded statement, forecast and valuation rows consumed by the child. Mission, Constitution, Playbook, policy and model configuration changes remain part of the permission suffix. Old permission records are retired only within the same company prefix.

The coordinator remains single-flight. It skips quiet and held companies, launches the first runnable company with `--company-ref`, and returns a combined held result only after examining every company. The child still owns eligibility and every human checkpoint rule; scheduling does not reopen an approved or rejected gate.

Validation: all 59 Deep Insight gate tests pass, including company-local input movement, first-refusal fairness, all-held behavior, and unchanged-input no-relaunch coverage.
