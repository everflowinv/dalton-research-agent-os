# Model prompt review: foundation slice — 2026-09-11

Owner clarification is authoritative: understand current market thinking and
anticipate its next move. State a current judgement, the conditions under which
the main and alternative cases occur, their consequences, and the observation
that would change the judgement. Neither compulsory contrarianism nor an
uncommitted “both could happen” answer meets this requirement. Long-term
industry/business/moat understanding remains the foundation; it is not replaced
by a short-term catalyst checklist.

includes planners, human intent/agenda, classification/quality, Initial Screen,
dossier, industry framework, deep gate, model specification, memo, earnings,
zero-base, conviction, thesis impact, transcript polish, numeric extraction and

changing its producer prompt alone would leave a contradictory validator.

Source-read and model-call failures are separate from prompt quality. This
review does not claim a model read an annual successfully merely because the
instructions improved, nor does a proposed observation count as an active
tracking task without the normal admission receipt.
