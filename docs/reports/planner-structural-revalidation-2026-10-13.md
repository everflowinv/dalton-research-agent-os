The complete paid research plan is refused if any inquiry is invalid. Two legal source-bound instructions were blocked before they reached the ordinary admission queue: one directed-document strategy redundantly wrapped the same five fields around its complete inner strategy; one typed canonical question referenced a real open reflection-backlog head excluded from the display catalog of qualitative questions.

`planner-structural-revalidation:0.1` removes only a single completely redundant wrapper. Every outer field must be present and canonically equal inside; absent fields, conflicts, unknown outer fields and multiple wrapper layers remain errors. All inquiry order, targets, replacement bindings, directives and sufficiency judgments remain intact. The complete ordinary plan validator runs afterward; it cannot apply the good subset of a bad plan.

A missing displayed canonical question can be resolved only for a typed inquiry whose exact version/hash/company appears in the current open reflection backlog. The Core owner reader rechecks the actual current open head, then the immutable original same-lineage admission, question, source and principal. Withdrawn admissions are excluded. This is a source-bound question reference, not numeric-target equivalence, an inherited purchase permission or a reopened recovery slot. Ordinary current numeric admission and source permission checks remain mandatory.

A prior failed plan can be accepted without constructing a model only when a bounded recent-plan reader proves its original Work and its successful formal result/receipt, exact route/profile/policy, all existing admissions and settlements, and the identical current FULL-state hash and current mission binding. A displayed state cannot substitute for missing execution capabilities. Missing or unsettled fees, malformed amounts, failed model results, source/state/mission changes or genuine remaining validation errors cannot qualify. The original settled ledger amounts are retained; a conservative settlement does not certify actual supplier billing. There is no new Work, route or paid call. The existing owner stores the full validated plan under its original state and Work identity.

The fixed free-revalidation marker is remembered in the operational attempt ledger. Failed free verification preserves the original terminal refusal, timestamp, count and reason instead of converting it into a transient retry. Accepted plans use the ordinary unchanged-state replay on restart. The marker changes the coordinator execution signature, not the paid TASK or Work identity.

Current official quality feedback and the latest checked ForecastModel identity are actual planner inputs. Their bounded strong fingerprints now participate in the coordinator signature. The CLI's existing material planning hashes already include quality feedback and the model version, so a material change can clear the 7,200-second count-only hold. Identical records do not trigger another cycle or another paid permission. A new CompanyModelSpec pointer is not included separately because it is not this financial-state reader's input.

Validation covers the real Cockpit/Scheduler/result receipt/router/day-budget path, current-policy changes without fee or Work changes, restart and two-hour terminal holds, complete typed strategies and canonical owner reads, withdrawn/wrong-principal/stale-head cases, conflicting wrappers, missing fees, corrupted formal records and full-validator rejection. The original two failed plan snapshots are display projections; they are not claimed as successful complete-state production replays.

The fix is generic. It does not repair substantive bad research, manufacture references or facts, remove failed inquiries, change owner budgets or certify company delivery. Source/state changes use the existing normal planning path and its normal budget gates.
