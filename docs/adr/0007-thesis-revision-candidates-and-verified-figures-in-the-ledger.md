# ADR-0007: Automation may propose that a thesis is wrong, and a verified number may be a Claim

*2026-09-09*  ·  Status: accepted  ·  Owner decision 2026-09-09, recorded in [parallel-development-plan v1.0 §1](../reports/parallel-development-plan-v1.0-2026-09-09.md)  ·  Extends ADR-0001 (thesis admission is human-only) and ADR-0004 (§2 write scope, §6 the ADR-0003 B carve-out); narrows the refusal ADR-0003 B installed in `CandidateStagingStore.stage`

## Context

Two things the system now does well end in the same place: a wall it was built to have.

ADR-0001 made the first coverage thesis a human admission and forbade automatic revision until someone defined a revision contract. ADR-0004 kept that and drew the line at the object level: `autonomy.may_write` may never contain a thesis, whatever a mission says. Eighteen months of that was right, because there was nothing to revise against. There is now: the mission has 2,170 Claims, five published Initial Screens, four passed gates, and a claim-review lane (P10b) that already reads admitted Claims back against the originals they cite and retires the ones that are wrong. The system can tell that a Claim is false. It cannot say the sentence that follows — *and therefore the thesis that Claim supported may be weaker than we said*. It has no way to say it, so it says nothing, and the person who would decide never learns there was anything to decide.

The second wall is numeric. ADR-0003 chose option B: a transcript candidate enters staging as `claim_kind = qualitative`, carries no numeric authority, and the number goes back to a SEC Claim. That was correct for a transcript, where the only thing binding "3%" to reality is a text span. `CandidateStagingStore.stage` enforces it structurally: when `verification_mode` is one of `CITED_CORE_AUTHORITY_MODES` — `transcript_core_authority` or `public_web_core_authority` — a quantitative candidate is refused with *"a cited original is not a numeric authority"*.

But the mission has since built two numeric authorities that are not transcripts and are not text spans. `coverage_mission_document_figures` holds figures read out of a company-filed document, each one bound to a `quote_id`, its verbatim `citation_text`, the document's `source_manifest_hash`, an `as_reported_label`, a metric, a period, a basis and a `source_grade` restricted to `company-filed-document` or `earnings-call-transcript`; append-only, trigger-guarded, `verified_by` and `observed_by` recorded. `coverage_mission_statement_lines` holds 10,023 lines from 26 quarterly filings, each line reachable to a `coverage_mission_statement_filings` row that carries the SEC `accession`, the form, the filing date and the governance record the ingest ran under. The provenance on a statement line is *stronger* than on the SEC company-facts Claims that the policy lane already commits automatically. Both stop at the mission tables; the cockpit reads them directly, because they cannot enter the Ledger. So Dalton holds ten thousand verified numbers that no Claim query can see, and the reason is a rule written for a different kind of source.

Neither of these is a lane. Both are contract changes, and both change who may write what — which is exactly what an ADR is for, and why neither is implemented here.

## Decision

**Automation may submit a ThesisRevisionCandidate, and only a human may accept one.** The candidate is an append-only versioned record bound to exactly one `ThesisVersion` by ref and hash. It carries: the proposed change to the thesis statement (or `null`, when only confidence moves); the proposed `confidence` in ADR-0001's ordinal vocabulary (`low / medium / high`, never a float); the falsifier it hit, named by its ref in the active Driver Pack, when a named falsifier is what fired; the evidence refs and Claim refs that occasioned it, at least one, exact and hashed; and exactly one word from the Playbook's frozen `DECISION_VOCABULARY` — `NO_CHANGE`, `THESIS_STRENGTHENED`, `THESIS_WEAKENED`, `THESIS_BROKEN`, `NEW_THESIS`. It carries no authority to change anything.

**The candidate is the whole of the extension.** `thesis_revision_candidate` joins `AUTOMATION_WRITE_SCOPES`; `thesis` does not, and never will under this ADR. ADR-0001 §6 stands unchanged for the admission itself: no automatic revision, no automation-written `ThesisVersion`, no automation-moved current pointer. What changes is that automation may now *say* something, in a shape a person can decide on, instead of being unable to form the sentence. `thesis_revision_candidate` also joins `CHECKPOINT_KINDS`: a mission that grants the scope must carry the checkpoint, and refusing a candidate is as much a recorded decision as accepting one — a rejection with a reason is how the next tick knows not to re-raise it, and how a weekly review can ask what the machine kept proposing that the analyst kept declining.

**A `NEW_THESIS` candidate is a coverage admission, not a revision.** It routes to ADR-0001's existing admission path with its bindings (active mandate, active Driver Pack, template, driver refs, falsifier refs) and its human decision. Automation may name the gap; it may not shortcut the admission.

**A verified figure may become a quantitative Claim, and the rule that forbids it is narrowed, not deleted.** The refusal in `CandidateStagingStore.stage` today is *"if `verification_mode in CITED_CORE_AUTHORITY_MODES` and the candidate is not qualitative, reject"*. The rule change is to make that condition about the **authority the number comes from**, not about the fact that the original was cited. Concretely: the refusal keeps applying to `transcript_core_authority` unchanged, and a new `verification_mode` — `mission_figure_authority` — admits a quantitative candidate on exactly these conditions, all of which must hold and all of which are recomputed at staging time, not asserted by the caller:

1. the candidate's numeric spec resolves to one row of `coverage_mission_document_figures` or `coverage_mission_statement_lines`, named by its primary key and its content hash;
2. the row's `value`, `unit`, `scale`, `currency` and `period` are the candidate's, character for character — the staging store reads the row and compares, and a mismatch is a conflict, not a warning;
3. the row's own provenance resolves: for a figure, the `document_ref` and `source_manifest_hash` name a Core-held original and the `quote_id` names a span within it; for a statement line, the `ingest_id` resolves to a filing row whose `accession`, `form` and `governance_ref`/`governance_hash` are present and whose governance record is approved;
4. the `source_grade` is `company-filed-document`, or the line came from a 10-Q or 10-K. An `earnings-call-transcript` figure stays qualitative: ADR-0003's finding was about transcripts and it was right about transcripts.

**`public_web_core_authority` stays qualitative.** A fetched public page is a page someone wrote; nothing about the fetch makes its numbers an authority. It is in `CITED_CORE_AUTHORITY_MODES` today and it stays there.

**Neither change is legal until the governance policy is re-signed.** Both paths are auto-commit paths in the sense that matters: they end in a decision recorded against the active policy version, and `research_auto_commit` only authorizes a candidate whose rule ref is listed in `policy.research_candidate_auto_commit.rules`. So the figure path needs a new rule ref — `research-auto-commit:mission-verified-figure:v1` — and the owner has to publish and sign a policy version listing it, exactly as `research-auto-commit:mission-document-qualitative:v1` was signed for ADR-0005. The thesis path needs no auto-commit rule at all, because nothing about it is automatic; it needs the mission version that grants `thesis_revision_candidate` and requires the matching checkpoint, which is the owner's signature by a different route. Until both signatures exist, this ADR changes nothing that runs.

## Consequences

- The wall in ADR-0001 §6 is still there. Automation cannot revise a thesis; it can only put a decidable proposal in front of a person. If that proves noisy, the fix is a cadence or a threshold on the proposer, never a relaxation of who accepts.
- PROJECT_STATUS next-step 6 stops being a dead end: the figures in `coverage_mission_document_figures` and the lines in `coverage_mission_statement_lines` become answerable by Claim queries, and the two provenance paths converge on one shape instead of the cockpit reading two tables directly.
- ADR-0003's finding is preserved and made precise. It said a cited original is not a numeric authority; what it meant, and what the code will then say, is that *a transcript* is not one. A filed statement line with an accession and a governance-bound ingest always was.
- The staging store gains a read of the mission tables, which it does not have today — it is an owner-only scratch authority with no Ledger handle, and this gives it a second, read-only handle. That is a real widening of what the staging process can see and should be reviewed as such when it is implemented.
- The five-word decision vocabulary now appears outside the Playbook for the first time. It stays frozen; a candidate carrying a sixth word is refused by contract, not by policy.
- `gate_reopen` is in `CHECKPOINT_KINDS` and is deliberately not used here. Whether a passed Initial Screen should be re-issued on thicker evidence is a separate decision (see ADR-0008) and this ADR does not take it.
- Nothing here is implemented yet. The owner's decision is recorded; the figure half still needs a signed policy version listing `research-auto-commit:mission-verified-figure:v1`, and the thesis half still needs a mission version granting `thesis_revision_candidate` with its matching checkpoint. Until both signatures exist, nothing that runs changes.
