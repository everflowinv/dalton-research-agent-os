# ADR-0017: Source attribution, forecast normalisation and an honest return bridge. A Claim about financials must belong to the issuer whose table it cites; a model's base is cleaned of one-offs and its costs, shares and cash are held to invariants; an expected return is never built on a proxy the model refused

*2026-10-09*  ·  Status: proposed (owner-provided research artifact quality review of 2026-10-09, 「P0：三条会改变主结论的错误链」; owner direction the same day: 「要从源头上解决问题，确保以后在新的环境下研究新的公司时不再出现问题，不要针对个例打补丁」)  ·  Builds on P10b (claim retirement, append-only), P10x/2026-09-24 (span-level subject rule), 2026-09-26b (independent support check), the forecast coverage rules of 2026-10-09 (structure 0.6, `forecast_coverage*`), ADR-0014 (return attribution) and the owner decision on batched redrafts (research capability diagnosis 2026-10-09, item 4)

## Context

The review traced three error chains that change a main conclusion. None of them is a company-specific accident; each is a rule that was missing.

1. **Issuer attribution.** ws-7d held 22 Claims filed under MSFT whose cited passages are the financial-model pages of a Morgan Stanley note on Oracle (`ORACLE_20260123_0501.pdf`, compiled into the company wiki with `related_companies: [ORCL, MSFT]`). Every quote was exact and every hash verified. The subject checks passed because the wiki head names MSFT, so the document counted as "MSFT's own". The tables (license/support, IaaS/PaaS, FY25 revenue of about $57.4B, FY26E–FY28E columns) are Oracle's, and "latest-period free cash flow is negative" read Oracle's *estimates* as Microsoft's actuals. The initial screen cited 10 of them, the dossier 22 (13 in its body), the debate map 9, two scenarios one each.
2. **One-offs compounded.** META's model v3 compounded G&A +30.6% a quarter and R&D +13.95% a quarter from a base quarter holding a $2.4B legal charge and ~$1.18B of severance -- which its own spec called one-off -- to an operating loss of $31.3B in 2028 Q2. Free cash flow stayed +$18.6B and rising, because operating cash flow was 57% of revenue with no bridge to earnings. The first quarter sat above the company's Q3 revenue guidance.
3. **A proxy the model refused.** With net income unavailable, the return bridge multiplied model revenue by the trailing net margin: 54.75% for GOOGL (a TTM swollen by ~$99B of securities gains), 29.84% for META (whose model showed losses), giving "own expected returns" of 23.77% and 25.87%.

The same review flagged, in the same code: IBM's net income computed as operating income less a rate on operating income (no pretax, no non-operating items), and CTSH's share count shrinking 2.31% a quarter with no buyback behind it.

## Decision

Every rule below is deterministic, applies to every company in every environment, names no company, document or Claim, and is exercised in `tests/test_p0_error_chains.py` on synthetic companies (Acme Robotics / Northwind Logistics, Synthetic Widgets Co.).

### 1. Issuer attribution of Claims about financials (`claim_issuer_attribution`)

`attribute(text, span, statement, subject, issuers)` answers whose table a cited passage is:

- **Only statements about financials** (revenue, margins, cash flow, capex, debt, leases, buybacks...) are judged; a CIO survey naming the subject is not a table.
- **Primary issuer of the document**, in order: a front matter's `分类: TICKER (公司)` and its source file name; a title naming exactly one issuer; the density of mentions. A head that *lists* related companies is not a primary issuer -- that list is how the Oracle note became "MSFT's own".
- **Local context** is the head of the page the span sits on (table title, column headers) and 600 characters either side.
- **Verdicts.** `consistent` when the document is the subject's own or the subject is named locally; `misattributed` when the document's primary issuer is another company and the subject is not named locally (the table issuer is whoever the passage names, else the primary issuer); `ambiguous` when there is no primary issuer (a digest) and the subject is not named.
- **Period and actual vs estimate** are recorded with every verdict (`FY26E`, `2/27E`, `MSe` columns, and whether the statement reads them as reported results). Recorded, never a retirement reason on its own.

Where it acts:

- **Admission** (`document_extraction.admission_attribution_check`): a `misattributed` statement is held for a person with the rationale; an `ambiguous` one is asked of the independent support check (the cheap model pass) whatever its source -- the company wiki included -- with the issuer context as a document fact. The verdict is stored beside the admission as `attribution_evidence`.
- **Retroactive audit** (`ClaimReviewDriver.audit_issuer_attribution`, inside the claim review patrol every tick): admitted Claims not yet audited under the rule ref are read against their exact originals, newest first, at most 12 distinct originals a tick (`claim_attribution_audits` remembers what was read). A `misattributed` Claim is challenged and retired through `ClaimRetirementAuthority` under the new reason `issuer_misattribution`, with the evidence in the challenge record; the authority re-runs the rule on the exact original before it writes. Append-only; a reinstatement can still withdraw it. Without the mission's `claim_challenge` grant the audit reports what it would retire and writes nothing. `ambiguous` is never retired here.
- **Products** (`product_rewrite_flags`): after the audit, every product kind is read at its *current* version -- mission deliverables (initial screen, earnings preview, event note), dossier, debate map, scenarios, deep insight gate, industry framework, event judgements (the last 14 days; older ones are history, graded by outcome checks), zero-base review, thesis. A section needs a rewrite at once when it cites a Claim retired for `issuer_misattribution`, and for any other retirement reason once fewer than half of the Claims it cites still stand (bookkeeping lists such as a dossier's `unit_provenance` are not sections). Such a product gets an append-only `needs_rewrite` flag naming the version, each affected section with its retired Claims, cited count, standing share and severity (`immediate` / `threshold`), and `requires_material`: the subject's own disclosures for the latest reported quarter (8-K Exhibit 99.1 / IR release, call transcript, 10-Q/10-K, official IR metrics page), in source priority, for the material acquisition service. The existing retired-citation display stays. Nothing is redrafted here: under the batched-redraft policy the product lanes read `needs_rewrite(arrivals=...)` when they plan their next redraft, a flag is `awaiting_material` until replacement material of a required kind has arrived for its subject (`evidence_index.material_arrivals`), and the lane's next version answers it (a flag is open only while the flagged version is current). The scan is skipped while no retirement and no product version has been appended since the last one.

### 2. Forecast normalisation and invariants (`forecast_coverage`, `forecast_coverage_assumptions`, `model_forecast_driver`; generator `rule:structured-coverage-priority:4`)

- **One-offs out of the base.** A line the spec marks `behaviour: one_off` that is booked every quarter is a recurring cost with a one-off in it: it keeps its growth or ratio method (never held at zero), and its base is normalised first -- an explicit released/filed one-off amount when a reader holds one (probed by name: the numbers lane's `earnings_release_outlook.one_off_items`), otherwise every spike above 1.5x the median of the four quarters before it is replaced by that median. The first forecast quarter steps from the filed value to the normalised base; the `because` and `rule:one-off-normalised-base:1` record it. Discrete tax quarters (a benefit on positive pretax, or a rate 15pp from the eight-quarter median) are left out of the effective rate.
- **Operating-cost growth invariant.** An operating cost line may grow faster than revenue by at most 2 percentage points a quarter for at most 2 forecast quarters; after that it is capped at revenue growth + 2pp (`rule:opex-growth-invariant:1`) unless an analyst assumption sets that quarter.
- **Share count bounded by buybacks.** A falling share count may fall only as fast as the forecast buyback line retires shares at the company's own trailing shares-per-dollar; with no buyback line it is held flat (`rule:share-count-buyback-bound:1`).
- **Guidance bounds the forecast.** A revenue guidance range for a forecast quarter clamps the trailing or consensus value into the range (`rule:company-guidance-bound:1`). Ranges are read from the numbers lane's records by name (a `revenue_guidance_ranges` reader if one exists, else `earnings_release_outlook.latest_outlook` guidance items with a low and a high); without one the model's notes carry `guidance_bound_unavailable`.
- **Cash bridge.** Depreciation and amortization is a coverage cash leaf. When the model has net income and D&A, operating cash flow is derived -- net income + D&A + working-capital change + the company's trailing mean of everything else between earnings and cash (share-based pay, deferred tax). Otherwise a share-of-revenue cash flow that stays positive and rising while modelled operating income is deeply negative (margin at or below -5%) is unavailable from that quarter, with the contradiction as the reason; free cash flow follows (`rule:cash-bridge-consistency:1`).
- **Securities gains are not earnings power.** A non-operating line whose trailing four-quarter magnitude exceeds 5% of revenue is forecast at zero with the reason.
- **Sensitivity magnitude gate** (`forecast_sensitivity`): a what-if quarter whose absolute value exceeds 3x the largest filed quarterly revenue (the review saw -$292.1B of quarterly operating income) refuses the whole sensitivity projection with the reason (`rule:sensitivity-magnitude:1`).
- **Tax base.** Tax is a rate of pretax income and net income is pretax less tax (the coverage chain since 2026-10-09; verified on IBM below). A legacy driver model without a pretax line no longer feeds an expected return (rule 3).

The generator ref moves to `:4`, so every model built under the earlier rules is rebuilt once by the model lane (`pending_action` returns `rules_upgrade`); no owner step.

### 3. The return bridge (`return_attribution._driver_leg`)

- **No proxy.** Without four computed quarters of model net income the driver leg is `unavailable` with the reason (`reason_code: model_net_income_unavailable`).
- **Core earnings.** With net income, the leg compounds core earnings: operating income x (1 - the model's tax rate), the same rate on the trailing four filed quarters and the forward four. Non-operating securities gains and one-off lines never enter either side. A non-positive forward core is `unavailable` (`model_core_earnings_negative`).
- **Target-price legs named for what they are** (batch B finding): the consensus and sell-side legs are, by construction, target price over today's price plus the dividend yield -- EPS growth only splits that number and the multiple change is its residual. They are labelled 「一致预期目标价隐含回报」/「卖方目标价隐含回报」, run on one 12-month window from the price date, and show the two parts (`target_implied_percent`, `dividend_yield_percent`). The re-derived consensus period labels (`financial_period`, `consensus_estimate.period_consensus`) are wired in once batch B is merged.
- **Contradiction notice.** When the model forecasts operating or net losses within eight quarters, every available expected leg carries `contradiction` (and a note), the bridge records `model_consistency`, the cockpit shows a 「与模型亏损矛盾」 badge and the comparison cell says it. The comparison and risk columns keep their 不可比较/缺失 reasons.

## What happens on deploy

- The claim review patrol starts the attribution audit on its next tick in each environment. Replayed on a /tmp copy of ws-7d it retires 78 Claims: the 22 MSFT Claims of the review's evidence file (set-equal to the archived issuer-attribution audit's `bad_financial_claims`), 6 more MSFT Claims cut from a second Oracle model in the wiki, and 50 AMZN/META/GOOGL Claims cut from CoreWeave, Nebius, Oracle and Amazon pages of other issuers' notes. The 8 cross-company CIO-survey/disclosure Claims the review kept are kept. Legacy: none.
- On the same copy, `needs_rewrite` flags 11 products (all `awaiting_material`): MSFT initial screen (10 of the 22, seven sections), MSFT dossier (all 22; 14 in its body sections, the rest only in its `unit_provenance` citation ledger), MSFT debate map (9, three questions), two MSFT scenarios (one each), the META deep insight gate, the AMZN and META dossiers and the META screen (immediate, other issuer retirements), and -- by the standing-share threshold -- the industry debate map and industry framework. Legacy (no issuer retirements): the industry debate map, the industry framework, two deep insight gates and the event judgements of the last 14 days whose only cited Claim is retired.
- The model lane rebuilds each structured model once. META (replay, 2028 Q2 vs v3): revenue $106.3B (consensus first quarters, within the $61-64B Q3 range) vs $102.0B; G&A normalised to its pre-charge base $3.6B vs $47.5B; R&D capped from 2027 Q2 $43.3B vs $61.6B; operating income +$34.2B vs -$31.3B; tax at 11.4% with the two discrete tax quarters left out; operating cash flow derived from earnings; FCF +$8.7B vs +$18.6B; the driver leg rests on core earnings.
- The return bridges recompute on their next refresh: GOOGL's and META's current proxy legs become unavailable until their models are rebuilt, then core-earnings legs.

## Not done

- The segment bridge invariant the review asked for on GOOGL (segment operating income + unallocated corporate costs + All Other = consolidated) needs `economic_invariants.segment_sum` to treat `CorporateNonSegmentMember` / `AllOtherSegmentsMember` as bridge terms rather than as reasons a breakdown is not checkable; that file is shared with other invariants and is left to batch ar.

- The admission hold creates human review work for `misattributed` statements. The deterministic retirement covers what is already in the Ledger; a later batch may let automation drop instead of hold.
- One-off amounts from earnings releases (META's legal and severance charges) are read only when the numbers lane exposes them; until then the spec-flag spike rule applies, and R&D severance is not separated.
- The growth allowance (2pp, 2 quarters), the spike factor (1.5x), the deep-loss margin (-5%) and the non-operating materiality (5% of revenue) are module constants, not mission settings.
